BUILT FOR ROAD SAFETY

REAL-TIME ACCIDENT REPORTING

Users can report accidents with exact location tagging and photo evidence to alert the network immediately.

AI-ASSISTED ANALYSIS

AI analyzes submitted accident images and provides severity estimations and observation information automatically.

ACCIDENT HOTSPOTS

Verified accident locations are analyzed spatially to dynamically identify and track highly accident-prone areas.

DRIVING SAFETY

Drivers can use destination-based Driving Mode with Google Routes and receive proactive hotspot proximity warnings.

EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Nearby available ambulance responders can receive emergency alerts and update their response status in real-time.

POLICE VERIFICATION

Police stations can cleanly review and verify accident reports before they permanently contribute to hotspot intelligence.

ONE PLATFORM. MULTIPLE ROLES.

USER

Report accidents, select locations, navigate safely and receive live hotspot warnings.

POLICE

Review and verify accident reports assigned to the appropriate station jurisdiction.

AMBULANCE

Receive emergency alerts, navigate to incidents and manage emergency response status.

ADMIN

Monitor users, accidents, emergency activity and oversee all system operations.

WHEN EVERY SECOND MATTERS

SafeRoute helps connect accident reports with emergency response so responders can receive incident information and navigate toward the reported location instantly.
